About

I study what machine-learning models learn, how they fail, and what evidence is needed before we rely on them.

My research sits between model interpretation and applied machine learning. I build controlled experiments and reproducible tools to study learned mechanisms, evaluate scientific models, and test claims about AI systems.

I care about clear methods, bounded conclusions, and results that other people can inspect and reproduce.

Research Interests

  • Mechanistic interpretation of transformers
  • Scientific machine learning and partial differential equations
  • Climate reconstruction and model evaluation
  • Trustworthy AI and evidence-gated oversight
